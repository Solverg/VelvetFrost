# Product

## Purpose

VelvetFrost is a Windows desktop atmosphere app and a fork of [Snowhush](https://github.com/mbezruchko/Snowhush), originally created by Mykhailo Bezruchko. It brings interactive weather to the desktop and adds a small world of winter objects, visitors and collectibles. The product and releases in this repository target Windows 10 and 11.

## Experience

Snow, rain, autumn leaves and cherry blossoms bring movement to the desktop without taking over the user's work. The tray menu makes the effect easy to adjust or turn off. In the snow, users can build snowmen, grow and decorate trees, collect rewards and encounter occasional visitors. These are optional discoveries: the desktop weather should remain enjoyable without collecting anything.

## Voice and design

- Calm, warm and playful, with room for small surprises.
- Explain what happens in plain language and make interaction discoverable.
- Give users control over motion, visibility and monitors.
- Describe measured performance only when the measurement and test conditions are available.
- Credit Snowhush and its original author clearly; describe VelvetFrost as a fork.
- Describe the source as available under the noncommercial license, not as open source.

## Accessibility

Keep controls readable and usable with a keyboard where supported. Respect Windows animation preferences and make decorative motion optional. New product surfaces should aim for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and avoid making essential information depend on animation.
